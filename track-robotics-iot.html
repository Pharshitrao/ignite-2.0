🎯 Open Problem Statement Policy

No fixed problem statements are prescribed. Participating teams have total freedom to identify any engineering challenge within Robotics, IoT and Automation and present their own working hardware-software build.

Suggested Focus Domains Explore & Choose Your Problem

Use these overarching focus areas to guide your team's project concept:

Autonomous Ground & Aerial Robotics

Unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs), micro-rover pathfinding, obstacle avoidance, robotic arm kinematics, computer-vision payload handlers, and drone landing telemetry.

Industrial IoT & Predictive Maintenance

Vibration analysis on rotating machines, thermal hot-spot detection, smart factory automation, energy metering nodes, and edge fault classification.

Air-Gapped & Low-Power Mesh Networks

LoRa/Zigbee sensor networks, emergency telemetry relays, environmental hazard beacons, and decentralized wireless monitoring systems.

Assistive & Medical Automation

Bionic prosthetic limbs, exoskeleton actuators, automated triage dispensers, patient vital monitoring beds, and smart wheelchair navigation.

⚠️ Component & Hardware Policy: Bring Your Own Equipment

IGNITE 2.0 is a standard technical hackathon. The organizers do not supply microcontrollers, motors, sensors, or component kits.

  • Components & Kits: Participating teams must bring their own development boards (ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, STM32), motor drivers, sensors, breadboards, batteries, and soldering tools.
  • Venue Provision: AICTE IDEA Lab provides high-speed Wi-Fi, electricity plug points at each team bench, seating, project mentoring, and standard hackathon facilities.
