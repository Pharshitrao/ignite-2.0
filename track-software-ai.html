Suggested Focus Domains Explore & Choose Your Problem

Use these overarching focus areas to guide your software & AI concept:

Autonomous Multi-Agent AI Workflows

Collaborative multi-agent reasoning, automated research synthesis, code verification pipelines, autonomous customer triage, and agent-to-agent negotiations.

Offline-First & Decentralized Web Apps

Progressive web apps (PWAs), WebRTC peer-to-peer data channels, local storage CRDT syncing (Yjs), and mesh web applications operational without active internet.

Edge AI & On-Device Multimodal Models

WebAssembly / WebGPU model execution, audio spectrogram anomaly detection, in-browser computer vision, and privacy-preserving local LLMs.

High-Performance Real-Time Web Systems

Real-time collaborative dashboards, WebSocket event streams, geospatial disaster telemetry trackers, and high-throughput data visualizations.
